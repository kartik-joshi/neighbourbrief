How it works

From open data to a checked bulletin in every language

The same, in words

  1. Open data: Toronto Police Service crime (this year and history), traffic collisions, yearly counts, Neighbourhood Community Officers, community services and boundaries; City of Toronto 2021 Census languages.
  2. Code counts every number by report date in Toronto time, one per report and category; percentages only when both numbers are 10 or more.
  3. The officer adds their own words: this month's priority, what was done, and what happened since last month.
  4. AI writes plain-language sentences around the numbers and translates them; it never supplies a number.
  5. Every check: same numbers in every language, right category and percentage, right alphabet, same meaning when read back in English, safety wording, and optionally a second person's sign-off. Anything wrong blocks publishing.
  6. Publish: one printed page per language, WhatsApp and newsletter text, an image to share, large print, a static public site, feeds and an embed card.
